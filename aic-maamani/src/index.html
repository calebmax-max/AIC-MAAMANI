Our story

Made by hand, made with love

Viatas Bites started in a home kitchen with one mission: bring real, made-from-scratch cakes to everyday celebrations — no shortcuts, no preservatives, just good ingredients and a lot of care.

Today we bake for birthdays, baby showers, office parties and quiet Sunday afternoons — and every order still gets the same hands-on attention as our very first cake.

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

For custom celebration cakes we recommend at least 3–5 days notice, and 1–2 weeks for weddings or large corporate orders. For cupcakes and everyday slices, 24 hours is usually enough. Same-day orders are sometimes possible — just WhatsApp us and we'll tell you what's available.

Yes — we deliver to most Nairobi areas including CBD, Westlands, Kilimani, Karen, Langata, Kasarani, Thika Road and more. Delivery fees start from KES 300 depending on your area. You can also pick up for free from our kitchen in Nairobi West.

Absolutely — every cake is made to order. Send us your colour scheme, theme, photos for inspiration, or a written brief. We'll confirm the design with you before baking begins. All our flavours are also available as fillings, so you can mix and match tiers.

Yes! We have fully vegan options (including our Chocolate Orange cake) and gluten-free alternatives for most flavours. Please mention your dietary requirements when ordering so we can bake and prepare them separately to avoid cross-contamination.

We accept M-Pesa (till or send money), cash on pickup, and bank transfer for corporate orders. A 50% deposit is required to confirm your order, with the balance due on delivery or pickup.

Cakes are baked fresh for your order and are best enjoyed within 2–3 days at room temperature (in a cool spot, away from direct sun) or up to 5 days refrigerated. Frosted cakes should be removed from the fridge 30 minutes before serving for the best flavour and texture.
